# Use cases

Eight concrete things a person can do with Laplace's Hoard. The persona is
always the same: a developer in Madrid who builds local AI tools on
Windows, writes fiction on the side, keeps a large photo library, is
job hunting and sews costumes as a hobby. They write numbers the Spanish
way (`1.234,56`, `03/04/2026`) and talk to Faustus in Spanish or English.

Every file below is synthetic and is generated by
`scripts/uxtest_data.py` into `data-uxtest/files/` (gitignored). The
person scenarios are driven by `scripts/ui_walkthrough.py` (Playwright),
the agent scenarios by `scripts/agent_walkthrough.py` (real MCP over
stdio). What happened when they were walked is in
[USABILITY_REPORT.md](USABILITY_REPORT.md); the verdict for each one after
the fix pass (last walked 2026-09-23) is in its "Re-walk" section.

| # | who drives | in one line |
| --- | --- | --- |
| UC1 | person, ES | Where does my money go? Bank export → spending by category → chart → CSV |

| UC7 | agent + Daguerre | Which focal length do I use, and do I shoot at higher ISO on the phone? |
| UC8 | agent | How much generation speed do I lose per 1k tokens of context? |

## UC1 — Where does my money go? (person, Spanish UI)

- **Goal:** see what they spent per category in 2025 from their bank's
  download, as a table and a chart, and keep the result as a CSV.
- **Starting state:** fresh install, no datasets. The bank's "Descargar
  movimientos" file `movimientos_2023-2025.csv`: UTF-8 with BOM, `;`
  separators, dates `dd/mm/yyyy`, amounts `-1.150,00`, 6% of rows without
  a category, 1,930 rows over two and a half years. A second bank gives
  `extracto_cuenta_2025.csv`: Windows-1252, three preamble lines above
  the header, amounts `-1150,00`.
- **Steps:**
  1. Open the app (browser in Spanish). Go to **Datos**, press **+**.
  2. Paste the path of the file (Windows "Copiar como ruta" gives it
     between quotes), press **Registrar**.
  3. Read the profile: are the dates dates and the amounts numbers?
  4. In **SQL**: spending per category in 2025, biggest first.
  5. **Gráfico**: bar chart of that result. **Exportar CSV**.
  6. Register the second bank's file too. Next month, download again and
     register the same file to refresh it.
- **Done when:** the amounts are numbers without hand-written SQL
  conversions, the chart shows the categories in the order of the query,
  the CSV opens in Excel, the second bank's file registers, and
  re-registering refreshes instead of failing.

## UC2 — "Faustus, ¿cuánto me gasté en supermercados en 2025?" (agent)

- **Goal:** a correct, cited answer to "how much did I spend on
  groceries in 2025, how much is that a month, and what share of my
  salary is it?"
- **Starting state:** Laplace connected to Faustus; nothing registered.
  The user gives the path of `movimientos_2023-2025.csv`.
- **Prompts:** "Faustus, registra `C:\...\movimientos_2023-2025.csv` y
  dime cuánto me gasté en supermercados en 2025, cuánto es al mes y qué
  porcentaje de la nómina supone." Follow-up in the user's own notation:
  "¿y el 21 % de 1.234,56?", "¿cuánto es 1.000 × 3?"
- **Expected chain:** `data_list` → `data_register` → `data_describe` →
  `data_query` (sum of the grocery rows of 2025) → `calc` (÷ 12) →
  `data_query` (payroll 2025) → `calc` (ratio), every number cited.
- **Done when:** the model gets the sums without having to repair
  `'-1.234,56'` text in SQL, the follow-ups either compute what a Spanish
  speaker means or say exactly how to write it, and nothing is silently
  read the English way.

## UC3 — Job hunt: am I being ignored for remote roles? (agent + person)

- **Goal:** response rate overall and by work mode, whether remote roles
  answer less (Fisher's exact test), and business days since the last
  application.
- **Starting state:** `busqueda_empleo_2026.xlsx` with two sheets:
  *Aplicaciones* (96 rows; dates as Excel dates shown `DD/MM/YYYY`;
  salary as numbers, `"55.000 €"`, `"60-65k"` or blank) and
  *Entrevistas*, which has a title row and a blank row above its header.
- **Prompts:** "Faustus, con mi Excel de candidaturas: ¿qué tasa de
  respuesta tengo? ¿Me contestan menos en remoto? ¿Cuántos días hábiles
  llevo sin enviar ninguna?" Then, as a person, the same Fisher test on the
  **Estadística** screen with the 2×2 table typed in.
- **Expected chain:** `data_register` (xlsx → one dataset per sheet) →
  `data_describe` → `data_query` (group by *Modalidad*) → `stats`
  `fisher_exact` with the 2×2 table → `stats` `proportion_ci` →
  `data_query` (last date) → `date_calc` `business_days` (Madrid).
- **Done when:** the table can be passed to `fisher_exact` over MCP, the
  *Entrevistas* sheet has its real column names, and the business-day
  count excludes Madrid holidays.

## UC4 — Novel pace (person, English UI)

- **Goal:** words per month for a novel-in-progress, words per hour, and
  how many more sessions of about 350 words to reach 90,000.
- **Starting state:** `sesiones_escritura.csv`, a clean comma-separated
  log (date, chapter, words, minutes, place), 156 sessions.
- **Steps:** **Data** → register → SQL grouped by month → line chart →
  total words → **Notebook** `calc`: `(90000 - total) / 350`; `dates`:
  `today`. Also type, by habit, `1.000 * 3`, `3,5 + 2`,
  `pct(21, 1234,56)`, `3,5 km -> mi` and `x^2 - 5x + 6 = 0`.
- **Done when:** every cell shows either a result or an error that says
  how to write it; nothing is silently computed the wrong way.

## UC5 — Where did my two weeks go? (agent + Funes)

- **Goal:** hours per activity category (coding, browsing, writing…) in
  the last two weeks, with a chart the person can look at.
- **Starting state:** Funes's privacy export `funes-export.json`
  (`{"spans": [...], "file_events": [], "commits": [], "exported_at": ...}`,
  446 window spans with realistic Windows titles). The model driving
  Faustus is a local, **text-only** 27B.
- **Prompts:** "Faustus, con el export de Funes, ¿cuántas horas programé
  y cuántas navegué estas dos semanas? Hazme un gráfico."
- **Expected chain:** `data_register` → `data_query` (unnest the spans,
  sum `end_ts - start_ts` by category) → `data_chart` → an answer with
  the hours, the cite and a link the person can open to see the chart.
- **Done when:** registering the export does not flood the model's
  context, the chart call returns text only (no image unless the model
  asks for one) with a URL that opens the chart in Laplace, and the
  person can see that chart from the work log.

## UC6 — Everyday numbers (person, Spanish UI)

- **Goal:** quick answers without opening a spreadsheet: 21 % IVA on
  1.234,56 €, a costume measurement from an English sewing pattern
  (`72 pulgadas`, `3,5 km` of a walk, in miles), and the number
  of working days in Madrid until 30/10/2026.
- **Starting state:** any.
- **Steps:** **Cuaderno** `calc`; **Unidades y fechas** → Unidades
  (`3,5 km` → `mi`, `72 pulgadas` → `cm`) and Fechas → *Días
  laborables* (`hoy` → `30/10/2026`, Spain/Madrid).
- **Done when:** decimal commas are either understood or refused with
  the exact way to write them; Spanish unit names are understood or the
  error names the English unit; the business-day count lists the
  holiday it skipped.

## UC7 — What do I actually shoot with? (agent + Daguerre)

- **Goal:** the most used camera/focal length pairs, and whether the
  phone photos have higher ISO than the Sony ones (Mann-Whitney U).
- **Starting state:** the Daguerre library database (`daguerre-library.sqlite`,
  a `photos` table of 640 rows with EXIF columns and a small `thumb`
  BLOB), registered read-only.
- **Prompts:** "Faustus, mira mi biblioteca de Daguerre: ¿qué focal uso
  más? ¿Disparo con ISO más alto con el iPhone que con la Sony?"
- **Expected chain:** `data_register` (SQLite → one dataset per table) →
  `data_describe` → `data_query` → `stats` `mannwhitneyu` with
  `group_by="make"` and `where="make IN ('Apple', 'SONY')"`.
- **Done when:** the BLOB column does not flood the describe/query
  results, and the two-group rule is explained by the error when the
  model forgets the filter.

## UC8 — Context length vs speed (agent)

- **Goal:** tokens per second lost per 1k tokens of context for the
  27B model, from their own llama.cpp benchmark log.
- **Starting state:** `llama_bench.ndjson`, 1,200 runs of three models;
  about 1% of runs crashed and have `tokens_per_s: null`.
- **Prompts:** "Faustus, con `llama_bench.ndjson`: ¿cuántos tokens/s
  pierdo por cada 1k de contexto con qwen3-27b?" Then "¿qué calculaste
  antes?" (`work_log`).
- **Expected chain:** `data_register` → `stats` `linregress`
  (`column="ctx"`, `column2="tokens_per_s"`, `where` the model) →
  `calc` (slope × 1000) → `work_log` with the id.
- **Done when:** crashed runs (NULL) are dropped pairwise without the
  model having to guess an `IS NOT NULL` filter, and the earlier result
  can be looked up by its id.
